About

Five-plus years building backends where latency and volume matter.

Most of my work sits behind the UI: Spring Boot APIs, queue-driven workers, routing rules, and integrations that have to survive real traffic. I design for concurrency, failure modes, and fair routing — not only CRUD endpoints.

I freelance for teams that need that kind of backend thinking. The demos on this page expose their metrics on purpose, so you can judge the engineering before we start a paid milestone.

  1. Foundations

    Competitive programming — ICPC / ECPC

    Algorithms, complexity, and edge cases under time pressure. Still how I approach a slow query or a race condition.

  2. Volume

    Campaign & messaging systems

    Outbound pipelines sending 20M+ messages per campaign — provider quotas, retries, and making sure nothing is sent twice.

  3. Latency

    Detection & processing pipelines

    Reworked a production pipeline with concurrency and shared-resource design: ~45 minutes down to ~2.

  4. Cloud

    Multi-cloud data & messaging

    Backends spanning AWS, Azure, and GCP storage and messaging patterns, plus .NET Core integrations when needed.

  5. Now

    Freelance — Measurable Systems

    Building queue-driven APIs and integrations for teams that need backends they can measure. Every demo here exposes its metrics.
